# Risk Disclosures & Edge Cases

Options trading involves significant financial risk. Derivatives contracts are leveraged instruments where market movements can lead to complete loss of invested capital.

Before trading on Zenith, participants must understand the risk mechanics governing option buyers, option writers, and decentralized infrastructure.

---

<h2 id="writer-risk">Option Writer Risk (Short Gamma)</h2>

Selling options (writing contracts) allows market participants to earn upfront yield by collecting premiums. However, writers assume asymmetric risk profiles:

### 1. Covered Call Risk
* **Opportunity Cost:** If the underlying asset surges significantly above the strike price $K$, the writer's upside is strictly capped at $K + \text{Premium}$. The writer forfeits all further upside appreciation.
* **Underlying Depreciation:** The writer continues to bear 100% downside market risk of holding the underlying asset. The premium received provides only a modest buffer against market crashes.

### 2. Cash-Secured Put Risk
* **Crash Risk:** If the underlying asset drops to zero, the writer is obligated to purchase the asset at the agreed strike price $K$, incurring substantial capital loss:
<BlockMath math="\text{Max Loss}_{\text{short put}} = (K - P_0) \cdot N" />
* **Capital Lockup:** The writer's USDC collateral is locked in the Soroban contract until position closure or expiration, preventing re-allocation to alternative trading opportunities.

---

<h2 id="buyer-risk">Option Buyer Risk (Premium Decay)</h2>

Option buyers face total capital loss if contracts fail to reach in-the-money status before expiration:

* **Theta ($\Theta$) Time Decay:** An option's time value decays exponentially as expiration approaches. For short-dated options ($< 14$ days), theta decay accelerates rapidly:
<BlockMath math="\Theta \propto -\frac{S \cdot \sigma}{2\sqrt{t}}" />
* **Complete Loss of Premium:** If an option expires out-of-the-money by even $0.0001, 100% of the premium paid is lost. Unlike spot assets, options have an immutable expiration deadline.
* **Volatility Crush:** If implied volatility drops sharply after entering a position (e.g., following a major protocol announcement or CPI release), option values decline even if the spot price moves favorably.

<h2 id="oracle-risk">Oracle Latency & Deviation</h2>

Zenith uses the Reflector decentralized oracle for settlement. Traders should be mindful of key oracle constraints:

* **Update Cadence:** Oracle price updates on Stellar occur on ledger boundaries (every 4–5 seconds). Rapid spot market swings on centralized exchanges during intense volatility may create short-term price deviations.
* **Staleness Circuit Breakers:** If the oracle fails to produce an updated price within 180 seconds, contract settlement enters a temporary safety pause until fresh multi-source signatures are verified.
* **Median Outlier Rejection:** Extreme single-exchange flash crashes are filtered out by Reflector's multi-source volume-weighted median calculation.

---

<h2 id="smart-contract-risk">Smart Contract & Soroban Risks</h2>

While Zenith contracts are written in memory-safe Rust for the Soroban WebAssembly runtime, all smart contracts carry technical risks:

* **Runtime Upgrades:** Stellar network protocol upgrades (e.g., Soroban protocol version migrations) may cause brief network coordination delays.
* **Storage Rent Invariance:** Soroban introduces state expiration and storage rent. Zenith contracts automatically prepay state storage rent to ensure all option records remain permanently active through settlement.
* **Transaction Ordering:** On-chain transactions are subject to Stellar validator block proposal and consensus order.

<h2 id="portfolio-stress-testing">Portfolio Stress Testing</h2>

To help traders monitor multi-leg portfolio exposure, Zenith implements real-time scenario stress testing in `src/lib/risk.ts`.

The stress-test engine simulates instantaneous market shocks across:
1. **Spot Price Shocks:** Spot shifts from $-30\%$ to $+30\%$ in $5\%$ increments.
2. **Implied Volatility Shocks:** Absolute volatility shifts ($\pm 10\%, \pm 20\%$).
3. **Time Progression:** Advancing time by $1, 7,$ or $14$ days to isolate theta decay.

```typescript
// From src/lib/risk.ts lines 42-78
export function stressTestPortfolio(
  positions: Position[],
  spot: number,
  baseVol: number,
  spotShocks = [-0.20, -0.10, -0.05, 0, 0.05, 0.10, 0.20]
): StressTestResult[] {
  return spotShocks.map(shock => {
    const bumpedSpot = spot * (1 + shock);
    const pnl = positions.reduce((acc, pos) => {
      // Re-evaluate payoff across stressed spot conditions
      return acc + calculatePositionStressedPnl(pos, bumpedSpot, baseVol);
    }, 0);
    return { shockPct: shock * 100, bumpedSpot, pnl };
  });
}
```

Traders can access this directly in the [Portfolio Risk Panel](/portfolio) to ensure their positions remain within safe risk tolerances.
