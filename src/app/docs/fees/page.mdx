# Fees & Economics

Zenith is designed to offer the lowest cost derivatives trading in decentralized finance by leveraging Stellar's high-throughput architecture.

All fees are transparent, fixed, and enforced deterministically by the smart contract protocol.

---

<h2 id="fee-breakdown">Fee Structure</h2>

The protocol fee structure consists of two components:
1. **Protocol Trading Fee:** A fractional percentage fee levied on the option premium at trade execution.
2. **Network Gas Fee:** The sub-cent Stellar network fee required to record Soroban contract invocations.

There are **zero fees** for:
* Holding positions
* Depositing or withdrawing collateral
* Canceling unfilled limit orders
* Reclaiming collateral after expiration

---

<h2 id="trading-fees">Trading Fees</h2>

Trading fees are assessed as a percentage of the total option premium traded:

| Action | Fee Rate | Fee Cap | Beneficiary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Option Purchase (Taker)** | 0.30% of Premium | Max 12.5% of Option Intrinsic | Protocol Treasury / Liquidity Reserve |
| **Option Sale / Write (Maker)** | 0.15% of Premium | Max 6.0% of Option Intrinsic | Protocol Treasury / Liquidity Reserve |
| **Position Close (Before Expiry)** | 0.20% of Premium | Standard Cap | Protocol Treasury |
| **Exercise at Expiration** | 0.10% of Intrinsic Payout | No Cap | Keeper / Settlement Pool |

### Fee Capping Formula
To protect traders opening deep out-of-the-money (low premium) or deep in-the-money options, fees are dynamically capped:

<BlockMath math="\text{Fee}_{\text{trade}} = \min\Big(\text{Rate} \cdot \text{Premium} \cdot N, \; 0.125 \cdot S \cdot N\Big)" />

This ensures that trading low-priced or speculative options remains economically viable without excessive transaction friction.

<h2 id="soroban-gas">Stellar Network Gas Costs</h2>

Transactions on Stellar Soroban consume resources measured in CPU instructions and memory read/write bytes:

* **Stellar Base Fee:** Typically 100 stroops (0.00001 XLM), which equals less than **$0.000002 USD** per transaction.
* **Smart Contract Execution Fee:** An average Soroban options trade costs between **0.005 and 0.02 XLM** (approximately **$0.001 to $0.003 USD**).
* **Predictable Inclusion:** Unlike Ethereum or EVM L2s where gas spikes by 10x–50x during volatility surges, Stellar's fee market remains remarkably flat and predictable.

<h2 id="treasury-allocation">Protocol Treasury & Economics</h2>

Protocol trading fees collected in USDC and XLM are directed to the Zenith Protocol Treasury, governed by transparent on-chain rules:

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               100% Protocol Trading Fees                │
└───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
                            │
         ┌──────────────────┼──────────────────┐
         │ 50%              │ 30%              │ 20%
┌────────▼────────┐ ┌───────▼────────┐ ┌───────▼────────┐
│ Insurance &     │ │ Liquidity      │ │ Protocol       │
│ Safety Reserve  │ │ Incentives     │ │ Development    │
└─────────────────┘ └────────────────┘ └────────────────┘
```

1. **50% — Insurance & Safety Reserve:** Funds an on-chain reserve backing protocol security and extraordinary volatility buffers.
2. **30% — Liquidity Incentives:** Reallocated as trading rebates and volume rewards to active options writers and market makers.
3. **20% — Protocol Development & Audits:** Supports continuous smart contract audits, bug bounties, and infrastructure maintenance.
